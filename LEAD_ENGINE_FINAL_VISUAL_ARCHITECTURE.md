LEAD ENGINE FINAL VISUAL ARCHITECTURE v1.0
Product Identity

Lead Engine is NOT:

a CRM
a dashboard
a reporting system

Lead Engine is:

A Revenue Operating System

The founder should feel:

"The system is continuously ranking revenue opportunities and telling me what to do next."

PRIMARY VIEW
Revenue Queue

This is the default screen.

It must contain only:

Top KPI Strip
Weighted MRR
Weighted ARR
Opportunity Count
Conversion Probability
Calls Today
Messages Today
Queue Tabs
Opportunity Queue
Call Queue
Message Queue
Follow-Up Queue
Waiting Queue
Main Opportunity Table

Single table.

All queues are filtered views of this same table.

Right Intelligence Panel

Contains:

Why This Ranking?

Explains ranking logic.

Queue Revenue Summary

Revenue impact of current queue.

Founder Daily Brief

Compact version only.

Must not become a dashboard.

Maximum 3-5 insights.

SECONDARY VIEW
Lead Pool

Purpose:

Explore and segment all leads.

Contains:

Smart Segments
ICP Segments
Communication Segments
Hot Opportunities
Full Lead Database

This is not an execution screen.

This is a discovery screen.

SECONDARY VIEW
Revenue Analysis

Contains:

Revenue Forecast
Revenue Ranking
Revenue Risk
Revenue Recovery
Commercial Outlook

Pure analytics.

No execution components.

SECONDARY VIEW
Lead Acquisition

Contains:

Import
Airtable Sync
Import Results
Data Sources

No revenue execution.

TERTIARY VIEW
Founder Command Center

Must no longer be a major screen.

Contains:

Critical Tasks
Daily Plan
Communication Session
Operational Status

Founder Daily Brief must remain here in expanded form.

DESIGN RULES

Never create:

stacked dashboards
widget walls
multiple KPI sections
duplicated information
dashboard cards for decoration

Every screen must answer:

"What should I do next to create revenue?"

SUCCESS CRITERIA

If the founder opens the application:

Revenue Queue appears immediately.
Revenue opportunities dominate the screen.
Operational decisions are obvious.
No dashboard feeling exists.
No CRM feeling exists.
No analytics overload exists.
